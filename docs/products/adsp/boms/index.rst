.. _adsp boms:

Bills of materials
==================

This page hosts the bills of materials (BoMs) for the ADSP evaluation boards.

The EV-SC594-SOM, EV-SC598-SOM and EV-SC846-SOM are system-on-modules (SOMs) that can be mounted
on carrier boards. These carrier boards are listed separately below, with their own
BoMs.
