EVAL ADICUP3029
===========================================================================================

.. image:: images/board.png
   :align: center

Overview
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The :adi:`EVAL-ADICUP3029` is an Arduino-like development platform based on the
:adi:`ADUCM3029` ultra low power microcontroller, built around an ARM Cortex-M3
processor with integrated power management, embedded SRAM and flash, and an
analog subsystem that includes an ADC together with clocking, reset, and power
management. The board has an Arduino Uno form factor, two PMOD connectors, and
an I2C Grove style connector for easily attaching sensors and signal
conditioning add-on modules. On-board Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi
connectivity make it IoT ready out of the box, complete with software and
working hardware/software examples. It is programmed and debugged with the free,
gcc/gdb based CrossCore Embedded Studio, and works with a range of Analog
Devices add-on modules as well as 3rd party hardware.

Features:

- ARM Cortex-M3 based :adi:`ADUCM3029` ultra low power MCU with integrated power
  management, embedded SRAM/flash, and an ADC-based analog subsystem
- Arduino Uno form factor with two PMOD connectors and an I2C Grove connector
- On-board Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi connectivity — IoT ready out of the box
- Free, gcc/gdb based CrossCore Embedded Studio programming and debugging toolchain
- Broad ecosystem of Analog Devices shields, PMODs, and Grove add-on modules

Applications:

- Internet of Things (IoT) sensor nodes
- Wireless (BLE / Wi-Fi) data acquisition and monitoring
- Rapid prototyping with sensors and signal-conditioning add-on modules
- Low power embedded control and connectivity
